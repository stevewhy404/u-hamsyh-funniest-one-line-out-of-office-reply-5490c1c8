One out-of-office auto-reply line: "I'm out of the office until Monday. My inbox is unsupervised — please direct all urgent matters to the plant on my desk. It has seniority."

`main.py` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), deterministic, stdlib only.
